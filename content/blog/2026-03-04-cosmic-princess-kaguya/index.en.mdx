It was Wednesday, which is weekly-report day, but I watched *[The Super Kaguya Princess!](https://www.cho-kaguyahime.com/)* at a theater this week, so I wrote down my impressions.

Many people may already have written about the plot and clues, and many videos are already on YouTube.
I reviewed those public writings before writing this.
Though these are personal opinions, I also tried to keep some objective perspective.

## Pure Impressions

I found it interesting.
The character performances, voice acting, and Vocaloid-centered songs and ending theme were all very good.

There are elements that may only click for certain audiences.
I think the target age is around thirty to forty years old, including people like me who watched late-night anime and NicoNico videos during student years.
There were also many high school students in the theater, and it made me wonder how much of this film they can enjoy.
Not in terms of whether the story is boring, but in how many elements they can actually notice.
The cast, songs (Vocaloid tracks), and direction include many references that strongly appeal to Heisei-era anime culture.
I think it would be unusual for high school students to feel “this sounds nostalgic” or “I’ve seen this type of direction before.”

The story uses *The Tale of the Bamboo Cutter* as a base and incorporates time-leap elements from *Urashima Tarō* and *Tamakio no Monogatari*.
Time-leap anime is not limited to the Heisei period, but for my generation during student years, the following titles were broadcast:

- The Girl Who Leapt Through Time (Heisei 18)
- The Melancholy of Haruhi Suzumiya (Heisei 18)
- Steins;Gate (Heisei 23)
- Puella Magi Madoka Magica (Heisei 23)

I did not watch Haruhi live, but I likely watched Steins;Gate and Madoka in real time.
As I will mention later, people who watched those shows in their youth are likely the same generation that listened to these songs in student days.
There are many other Heisei time-leap works, but this generation likely recognizes many of the in-movie Vocaloid songs.
